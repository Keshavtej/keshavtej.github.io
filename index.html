About Me

I am a Site Reliability Engineer focused on scalable, resilient, and automation-first systems. I like turning messy operational work into repeatable patterns that make incidents easier to prevent, detect, and recover from.

My M.Tech work on auto-remediation with StackStorm shaped how I think about event-driven operations: detect early, enrich the signal, and recover with as little manual effort as possible.

Outside my day job, I stay active in the community through SREDAY Hyderabad, SRE Hyderabadi, and PyConf Hyderabad, helping create spaces where engineers can learn, share, and grow together.
