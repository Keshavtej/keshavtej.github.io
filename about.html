01 / Profile

I’m a Site Reliability Engineer focused on scalable, resilient, automation-first systems.

I like turning messy operational work into safe automation that helps prevent incidents, detect them earlier, and recover faster. My work sits across systems infrastructure, reliability engineering, and DevOps delivery.

My M.Tech research into auto-remediation with StackStorm shaped how I approach operations: detect early, enrich the signal, keep guardrails visible, and recover with as little manual effort as the situation safely allows.

Outside day-to-day engineering, I help create spaces where practitioners can exchange what actually works through SREDAY Hyderabad, SRE Hyderabadi, and PyConf Hyderabad.
